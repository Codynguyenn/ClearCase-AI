# Northwind Analytics External Sharing and Retention Guidelines (v1.0)

> **This document is a fictional example written for the ClearCase portfolio
> project.** It is not legal advice, does not reference any real statute, and
> must not be used to make real compliance decisions.

## 1. Purpose

These guidelines explain who may approve sending a redacted document outside the
company, how redacted files are labelled and kept, and what to do when something
goes wrong. They complement the Northwind redaction policy, which says *what*
must be removed.

## 2. Who may approve external sharing

A redacted document may be sent outside the company only after a second person
has checked it. The reviewer who prepared the redactions may not be the only
approver. For documents above the standard risk level, the Head of Legal must
also sign off in writing.

## 3. Labelling redacted documents

Every shared copy must carry the word REDACTED in its file name and on its first
page. File names must never contain the names or identifiers that were removed
from the document.

## 4. Hidden content and file metadata

Before sharing, remove document properties, author names, comments, tracked
changes, embedded attachments and form fields. Hidden text and metadata can
reveal exactly the information the visible redactions were meant to protect, so
a shared file is only acceptable once it has been rewritten without them.

## 5. Requests from courts, regulators and other third parties

Requests that arrive from a court, a regulator or an external authority must be
passed to the legal team unchanged. Staff must not decide on their own what to
disclose in response, and these guidelines do not describe any specific
statute or legal deadline.

## 6. Keeping records

The original document and the approved redacted copy are both kept for three
years after the matter closes. The original is stored in the restricted legal
archive; only the redacted copy may be circulated.

## 7. When an unredacted document is shared by mistake

If a document containing information that should have been removed is sent to
the wrong audience, tell the Head of Legal within one working day. Do not try to
recall or delete the message yourself before reporting it.

## 8. Exceptions

Any departure from these guidelines needs written approval from the Head of
Legal before the document is shared, and the approval is stored with the
matter record.

# Northwind Analytics Document Redaction Policy (v1.0)

> **This policy is a fictional example written for the ClearCase portfolio
> project.** It is not legal advice, does not reference any real statute, and
> must not be used to make real compliance decisions.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy describes which categories of information must be removed before a
contract or agreement is shared outside the legal team. It applies to all
documents reviewed in the ClearCase tool.

## 2. Personal identifiers of individuals

The full names of individual people who are clients, client employees, or
contractors must be redacted when a document is shared externally. This
includes names appearing in signature blocks, contact sections and notice
provisions.

Titles and roles (for example "Director of Operations") may remain visible when
they do not identify a specific individual on their own.

## 3. Contact details

Personal and business contact details for individuals must be redacted:

- email addresses,
- telephone and mobile numbers,
- postal and street addresses, including multi-line notice addresses.

A general corporate switchboard number or a generic department mailbox (for
example `legal@...`) does not need to be redacted.

## 4. Government and tax identifiers

Any number that identifies a person to a government or tax authority, such as a
social security number or taxpayer identification number, must always be
redacted regardless of context. A number that merely matches the format of such
an identifier should be flagged for review rather than assumed to be real.

## 5. Financial account information

Bank account numbers, routing numbers and payment card numbers must be redacted.
Monetary amounts, fee schedules and payment terms are **not** considered
sensitive under this policy and should remain visible.

## 6. Organisation names

Names of companies and other organisations are **not** personal information and
should remain visible by default. Redact an organisation name only when a
reviewer explicitly requests it for a specific document.

## 7. Dates and general terms

Contract dates, durations, governing-law clauses and other general commercial
terms are not sensitive under this policy.

## 8. Review requirements

Automated suggestions are proposals only. A human reviewer must approve each
redaction before a document is exported. Reviewers should prefer redacting a
specific occurrence over all occurrences when the same text appears in both a
sensitive and a non-sensitive context.

## 9. Topics this policy does not address

This policy does not cover health information, biometric data, criminal
history, or documents in languages other than English. Questions about those
topics should be referred to the legal team rather than answered from this
document.

## Limits

Synthetic one- and two-page documents from one author; a simulated reviewer who never errs; in-process timing on one machine, so network and large files are not represented; the verifier checks what was approved and cannot find sensitive text nobody proposed; scanned, mixed and encrypted PDFs are covered by the test suite, not measured here.

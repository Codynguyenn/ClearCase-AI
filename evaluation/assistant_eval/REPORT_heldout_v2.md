## Limits of this evaluation

12 short synthetic documents written by one author (who also wrote the policies and the prompt); one language; one run per document and model, so run-to-run variance is not measured; three injected documents is a demonstration, not a rate; the keep rule covers company names only; the labels call every address sensitive although 8 lines belong to companies (see the post-hoc section); the task is easy (one-page documents, clean text), so it cannot tell strong models apart; prices and model ids change; ground truth is the author's definition of "personal information". This says nothing about real documents.
